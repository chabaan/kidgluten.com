They have a higher melting point and a naturally sweet, slightly nutty flavor that kids love. If your family insists on chocolate, look for dark chocolate chips with at least 70% cocoa—they hold up better than milk chocolate in warm weather. Even then, keep the mix in the shade or in a cooler until you're ready to eat.</p><p>Another trick: <strong>freeze the trail mix before you leave</strong>. Break pretzel sticks in half and chop larger dried fruits into bite-size bits. Use roasted pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and soy nuts for crunch. Check labels on cereals and pretzels to make sure they're processed in a nut-free facility if that's a concern. A half-cup serving is about right for a snack.</p><p>If you're hiking, pack the trail mix in the side pocket of your backpack, not at the bottom where it'll get squished.
